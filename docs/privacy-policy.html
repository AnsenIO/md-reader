SquadShelf — Privacy Policy

Effective: September 3, 2026 · App: SquadShelf (Markdown reader, Android)

What SquadShelf does

SquadShelf is a local-first Markdown reader. It opens .md files you tap in a file manager, and includes an in-app browser for files in your device's shared Downloads folder. Everything you read stays on your device.

What data we collect

None. SquadShelf v0.1 contains no analytics, no tracking SDKs, and no account system. We do not collect, store, transmit, or sell personal data, and we have no servers that receive your files or usage information.

Permissions

  • Internet access — required by the embedded web engine (Capacitor) to serve the app interface locally. The app itself makes no outbound network requests.
  • Read external storage (Android 12 and earlier only) — used only by the in-app file browser to list and open files you select. On Android 13+, file access goes through the system's per-file picker and needs no broad storage permission.

Third parties

None. The app embeds no advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting services.

Changes

If a future release adds a feature that involves data collection, this page will be updated before that release ships.
