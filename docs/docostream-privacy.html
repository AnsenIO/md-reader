Privacy Policy — DocOStream Scanner

Last updated: September 5, 2026 · Publisher: IABAI (SquadShelf family) · Contact: support@squadshelf.com

The short version

DocOStream Scanner works entirely on your device. We collect nothing. Your scans, extracted text, PDFs and CSVs never leave your phone unless you export them yourself.

What the app does on your device

  • Camera — used only to capture document photos when you start a scan. Images are processed on-device.
  • On-device OCR — text recognition runs locally (bundled ML Kit Latin model). No image or text is sent to any server.
  • Files — PDF/CSV export uses Android's system save dialog; files are written where you choose.

Data we do NOT collect

  • No analytics, no telemetry, no crash-reporting SDK
  • No account, no sign-in, no advertising ID
  • No network requests of any kind — the app has no server to talk to
  • No access to your files beyond what you explicitly save or scan

Permissions

  • CAMERA — required to photograph documents. Not used in the background; no images are stored or transmitted beyond the scan session.

Data retention

Nothing to retain — no data reaches us. Files you export live in the folder you chose, under your control.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new date.
